Welcome to Our Finishing and Interior Services

At UMA, we understand that the beauty of a home lies in the details. That's why we offer comprehensive finishing and interior services to transform your space into a haven that reflects your style and personality.

Our Services Include:

  1. Interior Design:

    Our team of experienced interior designers will work closely with you to create a space that is both functional and aesthetically pleasing. From conceptualization to execution, we ensure that every detail is meticulously planned and executed to perfection.

  2. Finishing Touches:

    Whether it's adding crown molding, installing custom cabinetry, or selecting the perfect paint colors, our experts will add those finishing touches that elevate your home to the next level.

  3. Flooring Solutions:

    Choose from a wide range of flooring options including hardwood, laminate, tile, and carpet to find the perfect match for your space. Our skilled craftsmen will ensure a flawless installation that enhances the beauty of your home.

  4. Lighting Design:

    Lighting plays a crucial role in setting the mood and ambiance of a room. Let our lighting specialists help you design a lighting scheme that enhances the beauty of your space while providing functionality and comfort.

  5. Furniture Selection:

    Whether you're looking for a statement piece or need help furnishing an entire room, our interior designers can assist you in selecting furniture that complements your style and fits your budget.
